# Day 1: Open the SOC

A security operations centre is the team that checks warning signs and coordinates a response. Think of it as a hospital control room for digital services.

## Your mission

The hospital CFO approves a small security pilot. Establish identities, scope, and a spending trail before any telemetry flows.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the purpose in business terms, complete the investigation/build steps, verify the result and document the limitation. Objective references: B01.

## Teaching day

| Session | Activity | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Plain-language briefing, technical concepts, instructor demonstration | 75 |
| Afternoon | Guided build/investigation and validation | 150 |
| Close | Break/fix, evidence, cost and cleanup | 45 |
