# Day 10: Identity crosses the perimeter

An attacker may use a valid account or a connected business application. Changing a password can help, but the investigation also needs to check active access and application permissions.

## Your mission

A receptionist account appears in a SaaS alert and an identity alert. Decide what to revoke and why.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the purpose in business terms, complete the investigation/build steps, verify the result and document the limitation. Objective references: E04, E05, E06.

## Teaching day

| Session | Activity | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Plain-language briefing, technical concepts, instructor demonstration | 75 |
| Afternoon | Guided build/investigation and validation | 150 |
| Close | Break/fix, evidence, cost and cleanup | 45 |
