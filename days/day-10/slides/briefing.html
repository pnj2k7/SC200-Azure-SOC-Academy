Day 10 / Morning

Why this matters

An attacker may use a valid account or a connected business application. Changing a password can help, but the investigation also needs to check active access and application permissions.

Day 10 / Morning

The mission

A receptionist account appears in a SaaS alert and an identity alert. Decide what to revoke and why.

Day 10 / Morning

Key vocabulary

Session: an ongoing signed-in interaction. Token: a digital access credential. OAuth consent: permission for an application to act within an agreed scope.

Day 10 / Morning

Technical explanation

Entra Identity Protection, Defender for Identity and Cloud Apps see different parts of an attack. Session revocation is not a guarantee that every access token disappears instantly; verify behavior and residual access.

Day 10 / Afternoon

Practical access

Entra ID risk investigation entitlement (often P2), Defender for Cloud Apps; optional Defender for Identity with supported AD sensor lab. Licensed or simulation.

Day 10 / Afternoon

Demonstrate and verify

An identity/SaaS response plan with distinct Entra, Cloud Apps and on-premises Identity evidence and rollback.

Day 10 / Afternoon

Controlled fault

Join on display name instead of object ID in the packet, merging two people with the same name.

Day 10 / Close

Explain the outcome

Investigate existing sessions, authentication methods, OAuth grants and app permissions. Some access can persist independently of the old password.
