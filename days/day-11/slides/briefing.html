Day 11 / Morning

Why this matters

Finding a file and proving that someone downloaded it are different tasks. Use the right record for each question and collect only the information the investigation needs.

Day 11 / Morning

The mission

A fictional export might contain patient identifiers. Investigate audit, content, Graph and Purview signals without touching real records.

Day 11 / Morning

Key vocabulary

Audit: a history of recorded actions. eDiscovery: authorised searching and preservation of business information. DLP: controls designed to reduce inappropriate disclosure.

Day 11 / Morning

Technical explanation

Purview investigation has privacy and authorization boundaries. Audit answers what happened, content search finds material, Graph logs show API requests, and alert context guides response. None substitutes for the others.

Day 11 / Afternoon

Practical access

Microsoft Purview Audit/eDiscovery and relevant DLP/insider-risk entitlements; Purview role groups and legal authorization in the lab. Synthetic data only.

Day 11 / Afternoon

Demonstrate and verify

Three distinct evidence types are used correctly: audit activity, content discovery and Graph request logs, plus a justified Purview response.

Day 11 / Afternoon

Controlled fault

Use a Content search hit as proof the user downloaded the file.

Day 11 / Close

Explain the outcome

A relevant access/download/share event tied to the actor and file, corroborating session/app context and an authorized interpretation of the event.
