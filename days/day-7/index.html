Day 7: Engineer a reliable detection

A detection is a repeatable test for a suspicious pattern. Like a smoke detector, it needs testing: sensitivity that is too high creates noise, while too low can miss danger.

Your mission

Turn the recurring failed-sign-in pattern into a tested signal, without flooding the SOC.

Learning outcomes

Explain the purpose in business terms, complete the investigation/build steps, verify the result and document the limitation. Objective references: D01, D02, D03, D04, D05.
