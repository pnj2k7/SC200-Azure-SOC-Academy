Day 5 / Morning

Why this matters

Endpoint protection watches a computer for suspicious behaviour. Configure its rules and permissions before allowing it to take action.

Day 5 / Morning

The mission

Prepare endpoint controls and safe automation settings before the first endpoint case.

Day 5 / Morning

Key vocabulary

Endpoint: a laptop, desktop or server. ASR: rules that reduce opportunities for attack. Audit mode: observe what would be blocked without blocking it.

Day 5 / Morning

Technical explanation

Protection policy, detection, notification and automated response have different controls. ASR audit mode measures impact before enforcement; entitlement and device support can change the available settings.

Day 5 / Afternoon

Practical access

Defender for Endpoint Plan 2/equivalent trial entitlement; isolated supported device; appropriate Defender settings/RBAC rights. Intune or supported policy management for ASR.

Day 5 / Afternoon

Demonstrate and verify

A healthy onboarded device, scoped group permissions and auditable feature/policy settings; simulation users submit the corresponding configuration worksheet.

Day 5 / Afternoon

Controlled fault

In the worksheet change a lab-only suppression to all devices. Identify the visibility loss without applying the change.

Day 5 / Close

Explain the outcome

Pilot in audit mode on a scoped group, review operational impact and exceptions, then deploy under change control with rollback.
