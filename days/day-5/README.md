# Day 5: Protect the reception desk

Endpoint protection watches a computer for suspicious behaviour. Configure its rules and permissions before allowing it to take action.

## Your mission

Prepare endpoint controls and safe automation settings before the first endpoint case.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the purpose in business terms, complete the investigation/build steps, verify the result and document the limitation. Objective references: A01, A02, A03, A04, A05, A06, A09.

## Teaching day

| Session | Activity | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Plain-language briefing, technical concepts, instructor demonstration | 75 |
| Afternoon | Guided build/investigation and validation | 150 |
| Close | Break/fix, evidence, cost and cleanup | 45 |
