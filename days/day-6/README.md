# Day 6: Investigate the workstation

An alert is a warning to investigate. A timeline helps explain what happened before and after the warning; containment limits further harm.

## Your mission

A benign simulation triggers a device alert. Build a timeline and contain the right entity.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the purpose in business terms, complete the investigation/build steps, verify the result and document the limitation. Objective references: A07, A08, F01, F02, F03, F04.

## Teaching day

| Session | Activity | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Plain-language briefing, technical concepts, instructor demonstration | 75 |
| Afternoon | Guided build/investigation and validation | 150 |
| Close | Break/fix, evidence, cost and cleanup | 45 |
