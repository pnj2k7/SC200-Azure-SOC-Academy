# Day 8: Automate with a brake

Automation gives routine tasks to a workflow. Begin with small reversible tasks, such as assigning a case, and keep important decisions under human control.

## Your mission

Tag and assign lab incidents automatically; make a reversible playbook visible in run history.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the purpose in business terms, complete the investigation/build steps, verify the result and document the limitation. Objective references: A10, A11.

## Teaching day

| Session | Activity | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Plain-language briefing, technical concepts, instructor demonstration | 75 |
| Afternoon | Guided build/investigation and validation | 150 |
| Close | Break/fix, evidence, cost and cleanup | 45 |
