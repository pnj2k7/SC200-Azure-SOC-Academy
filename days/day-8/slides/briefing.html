Day 8 / Morning

Why this matters

Automation gives routine tasks to a workflow. Begin with small reversible tasks, such as assigning a case, and keep important decisions under human control.

Day 8 / Morning

The mission

Tag and assign lab incidents automatically; make a reversible playbook visible in run history.

Day 8 / Morning

Key vocabulary

Playbook: an automated workflow. Managed identity: an identity used by a service rather than a person. Idempotency: repeating an action does not cause duplicate harm.

Day 8 / Morning

Technical explanation

Automation rules orchestrate incident actions. Playbooks are Logic Apps workflows with independent identities and billing. Retries can duplicate actions, so idempotency and lab-only scope must be designed.

Day 8 / Afternoon

Practical access

Sentinel Contributor; Logic Apps deployment rights; Sentinel Automation Contributor role assignment for the service; no email recipients beyond yourself.

Day 8 / Afternoon

Demonstrate and verify

A real scoped owner/tag change and a successful harmless playbook run, with separate permission evidence for service and workflow identity.

Day 8 / Afternoon

Controlled fault

Remove a required action permission in the design worksheet (or a dedicated disposable identity), then predict the exact failing stage.

Day 8 / Close

Explain the outcome

No. Check the Sentinel service permission to run the playbook, the workflow connection and the identity permission for the specific action.
