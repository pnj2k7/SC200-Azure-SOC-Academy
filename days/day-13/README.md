# Day 13: Run the incident bridge

A case connects the warnings, evidence, decisions and people responsible. It should let a colleague understand what is confirmed, what is uncertain and what happens next.

## Your mission

Join the invoice, identity, endpoint and data signals into a case the hospital can act on.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the purpose in business terms, complete the investigation/build steps, verify the result and document the limitation. Objective references: E07, E09, E10.

## Teaching day

| Session | Activity | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Plain-language briefing, technical concepts, instructor demonstration | 75 |
| Afternoon | Guided build/investigation and validation | 150 |
| Close | Break/fix, evidence, cost and cleanup | 45 |
