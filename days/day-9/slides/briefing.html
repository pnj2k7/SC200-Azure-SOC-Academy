Day 9 / Morning

Why this matters

An email’s visible sender name can be misleading. Investigators examine delivery and authentication records and then check what the recipient actually did.

Day 9 / Morning

The mission

A fake supplier invoice reaches a fictional employee. Follow email evidence into the cross-product incident.

Day 9 / Morning

Key vocabulary

Message ID: a reference to an individual email. Authentication: checks of the sending system. Quarantine: holding a message away from the recipient.

Day 9 / Morning

Technical explanation

Email investigation crosses delivery, authentication and post-delivery actions. A malicious verdict supports remediation, while blast radius depends on who received, clicked or executed the payload.

Day 9 / Afternoon

Practical access

Defender for Office 365 appropriate investigation entitlement, Exchange Online lab mailbox and scoped response roles; otherwise CASE-MAIL simulation.

Day 9 / Afternoon

Demonstrate and verify

A trace from message to entity and verified remediation outcome, or a simulation report that preserves the distinction between suspicion and compromise.

Day 9 / Afternoon

Controlled fault

Search using the sender display name alone and compare with the supplied forged display name.

Day 9 / Close

Explain the outcome

No. Correlate click, authentication and endpoint activity; record what is known and what remains unproven.
