Day 16 / Morning

Why this matters

A relationship map shows how accounts, computers and files connect. Historical searches look further back, while summaries reduce detail to make some questions cheaper to answer.

Day 16 / Morning

The mission

The same identity may have appeared weeks earlier. Compare graphs, historical jobs and summary tables.

Day 16 / Morning

Key vocabulary

Graph: a map of entities and their relationships. Data lake: storage and analysis capabilities for longer-term data. Summary: an aggregate that intentionally loses some detail.

Day 16 / Morning

Technical explanation

Graphs expose relationships; they do not establish guilt. Historical jobs run asynchronously and promote results; summary rules materialize aggregates. Their cost, permissions and output semantics differ.

Day 16 / Afternoon

Practical access

Sentinel data lake and graph availability/entitlement plus scoped roles for live advanced path; otherwise offline graph and job designs.

Day 16 / Afternoon

Demonstrate and verify

Evidence-backed entity graph plus verified job/summary output, or explicitly labelled designs with reproducible expected results.

Day 16 / Afternoon

Controlled fault

Day 16 / Close

Explain the outcome

Aggregation can discard entity IDs and fine-grained timestamps. Preserve the investigation dimensions or maintain access to raw evidence.
