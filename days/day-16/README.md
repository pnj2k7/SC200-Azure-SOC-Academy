# Day 16: Follow the historical trail

A relationship map shows how accounts, computers and files connect. Historical searches look further back, while summaries reduce detail to make some questions cheaper to answer.

## Your mission

The same identity may have appeared weeks earlier. Compare graphs, historical jobs and summary tables.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the purpose in business terms, complete the investigation/build steps, verify the result and document the limitation. Objective references: H05, H06, I02, I03.
