# Day 14: Make the SOC observable

A dashboard is useful only when its numbers have a clear meaning. A quiet chart could mean there were no events, or that collection stopped working.

## Your mission

The CFO asks whether detection is working and what the data costs. Build an honest dashboard.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the purpose in business terms, complete the investigation/build steps, verify the result and document the limitation. Objective references: B03, B04.

## Teaching day

| Session | Activity | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Plain-language briefing, technical concepts, instructor demonstration | 75 |
| Afternoon | Guided build/investigation and validation | 150 |
| Close | Break/fix, evidence, cost and cleanup | 45 |
