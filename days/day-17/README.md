# Day 17: Use AI with evidence

AI can help organise an investigation, but a convincing explanation can still be wrong. Ask for evidence and check the underlying records before making a decision.

## Your mission

The incident lead wants help from Copilot and notebooks. Validate every generated claim and keep tool access bounded.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the purpose in business terms, complete the investigation/build steps, verify the result and document the limitation. Objective references: E08, I04.

## Teaching day

| Session | Activity | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Plain-language briefing, technical concepts, instructor demonstration | 75 |
| Afternoon | Guided build/investigation and validation | 150 |
| Close | Break/fix, evidence, cost and cleanup | 45 |
