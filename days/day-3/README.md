# Day 3: Windows tells its story

A workstation can record successful and failed sign-ins, much like a reception access register. We choose which entries to collect so the register remains useful.

## Your mission

A reception workstation reports repeated failed logons. Collect useful Windows evidence without collecting everything.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the purpose in business terms, complete the investigation/build steps, verify the result and document the limitation. Objective references: C01, C02, C03.

## Teaching day

| Session | Activity | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Plain-language briefing, technical concepts, instructor demonstration | 75 |
| Afternoon | Guided build/investigation and validation | 150 |
| Close | Break/fix, evidence, cost and cleanup | 45 |
