Day 18 / Morning

Why this matters

The final exercise asks you to explain an incident as a responsible colleague would: use evidence, recognise uncertainty, propose proportionate actions and prove that the lab has been closed safely.

Day 18 / Morning

The mission

An independent analyst must reconstruct the full case and defend a proportionate response before the pilot closes.

Day 18 / Morning

Key vocabulary

Acceptance criteria: agreed conditions for completion. Residual risk: the risk that remains after action. Sign-off: a recorded decision by the responsible person.

Day 18 / Morning

Technical explanation

The capstone tests reasoning under uncertainty, evidence quality and operational discipline. The best outcome is an accurate report with explicit gaps, even if some licensed capabilities remain Pending.

Day 18 / Afternoon

Practical access

All previous days or their explicit simulation alternatives; a peer/instructor is useful but not required.

Day 18 / Afternoon

Demonstrate and verify

A second reader can reproduce the findings, distinguish live from simulated skills and verify teardown.

Day 18 / Afternoon

Controlled fault

Choose two faults from the previous days and diagnose them from evidence without looking at the recorded fixes.

Day 18 / Close

Explain the outcome

When findings are reproducible, response outcomes and recovery are verified, uncertainty and access gaps are explicit, and costs/resources have been checked after cleanup.
