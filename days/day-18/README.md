# Day 18: The hospital incident exercise

The final exercise asks you to explain an incident as a responsible colleague would: use evidence, recognise uncertainty, propose proportionate actions and prove that the lab has been closed safely.

## Your mission

An independent analyst must reconstruct the full case and defend a proportionate response before the pilot closes.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the purpose in business terms, complete the investigation/build steps, verify the result and document the limitation. Objective references: Cross-objective capstone.

## Teaching day

| Session | Activity | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Plain-language briefing, technical concepts, instructor demonstration | 75 |
| Afternoon | Guided build/investigation and validation | 150 |
| Close | Break/fix, evidence, cost and cleanup | 45 |
