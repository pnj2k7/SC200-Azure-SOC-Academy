# Day 15: Hunt beyond the alert

Hunting starts with a question, such as whether an account behaved unusually, even when no alert has appeared. A good hunt tests both suspicious and harmless explanations.

## Your mission

There is no new alert, but the threat report suggests a suspicious process and a reused identity.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the purpose in business terms, complete the investigation/build steps, verify the result and document the limitation. Objective references: H01, H02, H03, H04, I01.

## Teaching day

| Session | Activity | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Plain-language briefing, technical concepts, instructor demonstration | 75 |
| Afternoon | Guided build/investigation and validation | 150 |
| Close | Break/fix, evidence, cost and cleanup | 45 |
