Day 12 / Morning

Why this matters

A weak door lock and evidence that someone entered are different findings. Cloud security tools report both weaknesses and suspicious activity; the response must distinguish them.

Day 12 / Morning

The mission

A workload protection alert points to the application server. Separate a posture weakness from an active incident.

Day 12 / Morning

Key vocabulary

Workload: an application and its supporting resources. Posture: how securely a system is configured. Sample alert: a training record that does not prove a real attack.

Day 12 / Morning

Technical explanation

Defender for Cloud workload protection detects threats; posture management prioritizes weaknesses. Both matter, but incident classification must follow evidence and resource context.

Day 12 / Afternoon

Practical access

Defender for Cloud enabled only for an explicitly selected lab workload plan; subscription security permissions; optional sample alerts.

Day 12 / Afternoon

Demonstrate and verify

A workload incident report distinguishes sample alert, posture recommendation, actual evidence and verified containment.

Day 12 / Afternoon

Controlled fault

Treat the secure-score recommendation as proof of a successful intrusion in the draft incident report.

Day 12 / Close

Explain the outcome

No. Verify the incident actions and residual compromise independently; secure score measures posture recommendations.
