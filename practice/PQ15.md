# Practice 15: Hunt beyond the alert

## Scenario

There is no new alert, but the threat report suggests a suspicious process and a reused identity.

## Questions

1. A hunt returns zero rows. What does it prove?
2. Which exact evidence would support your conclusion, and which alternative explanation should be tested?
3. What access, cost or business impact must the incident lead consider?

## Answer guidance

Only that the query found no matches in its data/window/scope. Verify coverage and query assumptions before claiming the behavior never occurred. Strong answers cite an appropriate source/event ID, scope and UTC interval, distinguish observation from inference and explain one uncertainty. Award 2 marks for the core explanation, 2 for suitable evidence and 1 for a proportionate operational decision. These are original learning questions, not exam dumps.
