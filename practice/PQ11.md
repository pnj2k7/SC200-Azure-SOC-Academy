# Practice 11: Find the data trail

## Scenario

A fictional export might contain patient identifiers. Investigate audit, content, Graph and Purview signals without touching real records.

## Questions

1. A content search finds a sensitive marker. What additional evidence supports an exfiltration claim?
2. Which exact evidence would support your conclusion, and which alternative explanation should be tested?
3. What access, cost or business impact must the incident lead consider?

## Answer guidance

A relevant access/download/share event tied to the actor and file, corroborating session/app context and an authorized interpretation of the event. Strong answers cite an appropriate source/event ID, scope and UTC interval, distinguish observation from inference and explain one uncertainty. Award 2 marks for the core explanation, 2 for suitable evidence and 1 for a proportionate operational decision. These are original learning questions, not exam dumps.
