# Practice 14: Make the SOC observable

## Scenario

The CFO asks whether detection is working and what the data costs. Build an honest dashboard.

## Questions

1. A source volume chart is flat. What additional panel helps decide if this is healthy?
2. Which exact evidence would support your conclusion, and which alternative explanation should be tested?
3. What access, cost or business impact must the incident lead consider?

## Answer guidance

Last event/ingestion time, connector health and expected source activity, alongside detection execution and incident outcomes. Strong answers cite an appropriate source/event ID, scope and UTC interval, distinguish observation from inference and explain one uncertainty. Award 2 marks for the core explanation, 2 for suitable evidence and 1 for a proportionate operational decision. These are original learning questions, not exam dumps.
