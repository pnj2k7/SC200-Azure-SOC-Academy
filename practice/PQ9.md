# Practice 9: The suspicious invoice

## Scenario

A fake supplier invoice reaches a fictional employee. Follow email evidence into the cross-product incident.

## Questions

1. Does delivery of a suspicious message prove the recipient is compromised?
2. Which exact evidence would support your conclusion, and which alternative explanation should be tested?
3. What access, cost or business impact must the incident lead consider?

## Answer guidance

No. Correlate click, authentication and endpoint activity; record what is known and what remains unproven. Strong answers cite an appropriate source/event ID, scope and UTC interval, distinguish observation from inference and explain one uncertainty. Award 2 marks for the core explanation, 2 for suitable evidence and 1 for a proportionate operational decision. These are original learning questions, not exam dumps.
