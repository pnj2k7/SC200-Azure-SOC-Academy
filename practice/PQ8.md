# Practice 8: Automate with a brake

## Scenario

Tag and assign lab incidents automatically; make a reversible playbook visible in run history.

## Questions

1. The Sentinel rule ran but the playbook action failed with 403. Is Contributor on the analyst enough?
2. Which exact evidence would support your conclusion, and which alternative explanation should be tested?
3. What access, cost or business impact must the incident lead consider?

## Answer guidance

No. Check the Sentinel service permission to run the playbook, the workflow connection and the identity permission for the specific action. Strong answers cite an appropriate source/event ID, scope and UTC interval, distinguish observation from inference and explain one uncertainty. Award 2 marks for the core explanation, 2 for suitable evidence and 1 for a proportionate operational decision. These are original learning questions, not exam dumps.
