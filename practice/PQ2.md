# Practice 2: Build the evidence room

## Scenario

Create the Sentinel workspace and capture the first harmless cloud control-plane change.

## Questions

1. The tag changed but the query returns nothing. What do you check before widening collection?
2. Which exact evidence would support your conclusion, and which alternative explanation should be tested?
3. What access, cost or business impact must the incident lead consider?

## Answer guidance

Confirm subscription diagnostics, destination workspace, UTC time window, ingestion delay and caller permissions. Inspect connector health before assuming an attack hid the event. Strong answers cite an appropriate source/event ID, scope and UTC interval, distinguish observation from inference and explain one uncertainty. Award 2 marks for the core explanation, 2 for suitable evidence and 1 for a proportionate operational decision. These are original learning questions, not exam dumps.
