# Practice 12: Defend the cloud workload

## Scenario

A workload protection alert points to the application server. Separate a posture weakness from an active incident.

## Questions

1. Can a higher secure score prove that yesterday’s workload incident is remediated?
2. Which exact evidence would support your conclusion, and which alternative explanation should be tested?
3. What access, cost or business impact must the incident lead consider?

## Answer guidance

No. Verify the incident actions and residual compromise independently; secure score measures posture recommendations. Strong answers cite an appropriate source/event ID, scope and UTC interval, distinguish observation from inference and explain one uncertainty. Award 2 marks for the core explanation, 2 for suitable evidence and 1 for a proportionate operational decision. These are original learning questions, not exam dumps.
