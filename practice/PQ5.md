# Practice 5: Protect the reception desk

## Scenario

Prepare endpoint controls and safe automation settings before the first endpoint case.

## Questions

1. Should an ASR rule be put into block mode across the hospital on first use?
2. Which exact evidence would support your conclusion, and which alternative explanation should be tested?
3. What access, cost or business impact must the incident lead consider?

## Answer guidance

Pilot in audit mode on a scoped group, review operational impact and exceptions, then deploy under change control with rollback. Strong answers cite an appropriate source/event ID, scope and UTC interval, distinguish observation from inference and explain one uncertainty. Award 2 marks for the core explanation, 2 for suitable evidence and 1 for a proportionate operational decision. These are original learning questions, not exam dumps.
