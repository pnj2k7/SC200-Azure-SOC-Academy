# Practice 13: Run the incident bridge

## Scenario

Join the invoice, identity, endpoint and data signals into a case the hospital can act on.

## Questions

1. Two alerts share an IP. Is that enough to declare lateral movement?
2. Which exact evidence would support your conclusion, and which alternative explanation should be tested?
3. What access, cost or business impact must the incident lead consider?

## Answer guidance

No. NAT/proxies and shared infrastructure create coincidences. Seek host, identity, process/session and time evidence supporting the movement. Strong answers cite an appropriate source/event ID, scope and UTC interval, distinguish observation from inference and explain one uncertainty. Award 2 marks for the core explanation, 2 for suitable evidence and 1 for a proportionate operational decision. These are original learning questions, not exam dumps.
