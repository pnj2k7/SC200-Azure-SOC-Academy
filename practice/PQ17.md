# Practice 17: Use AI with evidence

## Scenario

The incident lead wants help from Copilot and notebooks. Validate every generated claim and keep tool access bounded.

## Questions

1. Can a fluent Copilot narrative replace original event references in the incident report?
2. Which exact evidence would support your conclusion, and which alternative explanation should be tested?
3. What access, cost or business impact must the incident lead consider?

## Answer guidance

No. Verify entity/time/action claims against original records, preserve uncertainty and obtain the required human approval for consequential actions. Strong answers cite an appropriate source/event ID, scope and UTC interval, distinguish observation from inference and explain one uncertainty. Award 2 marks for the core explanation, 2 for suitable evidence and 1 for a proportionate operational decision. These are original learning questions, not exam dumps.
