# Practice 16: Follow the historical trail

## Scenario

The same identity may have appeared weeks earlier. Compare graphs, historical jobs and summary tables.

## Questions

1. Why can a summary table make an investigation impossible even while reducing query costs?
2. Which exact evidence would support your conclusion, and which alternative explanation should be tested?
3. What access, cost or business impact must the incident lead consider?

## Answer guidance

Aggregation can discard entity IDs and fine-grained timestamps. Preserve the investigation dimensions or maintain access to raw evidence. Strong answers cite an appropriate source/event ID, scope and UTC interval, distinguish observation from inference and explain one uncertainty. Award 2 marks for the core explanation, 2 for suitable evidence and 1 for a proportionate operational decision. These are original learning questions, not exam dumps.
