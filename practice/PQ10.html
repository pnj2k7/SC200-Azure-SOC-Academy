Practice 10: Identity crosses the perimeter

Scenario

A receptionist account appears in a SaaS alert and an identity alert. Decide what to revoke and why.

Questions

  1. Why does resetting a password alone leave an incomplete SaaS response?
  2. Which exact evidence would support your conclusion, and which alternative explanation should be tested?
  3. What access, cost or business impact must the incident lead consider?

Answer guidance

Investigate existing sessions, authentication methods, OAuth grants and app permissions. Some access can persist independently of the old password. Strong answers cite an appropriate source/event ID, scope and UTC interval, distinguish observation from inference and explain one uncertainty. Award 2 marks for the core explanation, 2 for suitable evidence and 1 for a proportionate operational decision. These are original learning questions, not exam dumps.
