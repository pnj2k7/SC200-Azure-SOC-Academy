# Practice 6: Investigate the workstation

## Scenario

A benign simulation triggers a device alert. Build a timeline and contain the right entity.

## Questions

1. Why is an isolation action marked submitted insufficient to close the case?
2. Which exact evidence would support your conclusion, and which alternative explanation should be tested?
3. What access, cost or business impact must the incident lead consider?

## Answer guidance

Submitted is not completed. Check the action outcome, device reachability/last seen, residual identity risk and recovery before closure. Strong answers cite an appropriate source/event ID, scope and UTC interval, distinguish observation from inference and explain one uncertainty. Award 2 marks for the core explanation, 2 for suitable evidence and 1 for a proportionate operational decision. These are original learning questions, not exam dumps.
