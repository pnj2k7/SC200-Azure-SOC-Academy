# Practice 1: Open the SOC

## Scenario

The hospital CFO approves a small security pilot. Establish identities, scope, and a spending trail before any telemetry flows.

## Questions

1. Does a subscription budget stop a runaway ingestion pipeline?
2. Which exact evidence would support your conclusion, and which alternative explanation should be tested?
3. What access, cost or business impact must the incident lead consider?

No. It warns, and billing data can lag. Limit data sources, monitor usage, and stop chargeable resources yourself. Strong answers cite an appropriate source/event ID, scope and UTC interval, distinguish observation from inference and explain one uncertainty. Award 2 marks for the core explanation, 2 for suitable evidence and 1 for a proportionate operational decision. These are original learning questions, not exam dumps.
