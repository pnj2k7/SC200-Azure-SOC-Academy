# Practice 18: The hospital incident exercise

## Scenario

An independent analyst must reconstruct the full case and defend a proportionate response before the pilot closes.

## Questions

1. When is the capstone ready for sign-off?
2. Which exact evidence would support your conclusion, and which alternative explanation should be tested?
3. What access, cost or business impact must the incident lead consider?

## Answer guidance

When findings are reproducible, response outcomes and recovery are verified, uncertainty and access gaps are explicit, and costs/resources have been checked after cleanup. Strong answers cite an appropriate source/event ID, scope and UTC interval, distinguish observation from inference and explain one uncertainty. Award 2 marks for the core explanation, 2 for suitable evidence and 1 for a proportionate operational decision. These are original learning questions, not exam dumps.
